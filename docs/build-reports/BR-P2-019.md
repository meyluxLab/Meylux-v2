## 1. Executive determination

**Primary disposition: B — NO QUALIFIED ROUTE ESTABLISHED.**

The continued investigation improved the evidence base but did not establish one compatible route that authoritatively supplies Funding, Open Interest (OI), and direct Basis for both exact required instrument scopes, BTCUSDT and SOLUSDT, with compatible units, timestamps, coverage, provenance, and existing-contract representation.

The earlier Binance USDⓈ-M OI gap remains unresolved. The inspected official USDⓈ-M schema still labels current openInterest and historical sumOpenInterest / sumOpenInterestValue without defining their unit or calculation/valuation basis. Official Binance product references do exist for SOLUSDT USDⓈ-M perpetual futures, and Binance’s funding FAQ explicitly names SOLUSDT in its funding-rate material. These sources improve the product-specific evidence, but do not provide the required authoritative unit definition for OI or one exact SOLUSDT-specific documentary chain for all three metric routes. They also do not establish current listing/runtime availability, which remains outside this Task Order.

Additional candidates were investigated using official provider documentation:

- **Bybit:** Funding history and historical OI are documented, and the ticker schema includes basis and basisRate. However, the ticker schema does not establish the precise meaning/units and reliable non-empty historical Basis coverage for the exact BTCUSDT and SOLUSDT perpetual products. The ticker’s response-level time does not establish a metric-specific Basis event timestamp.
- **OKX:** Funding history and explicit OI fields in contracts, coin quantity and USD value are documented. The official premium-history metric is a premium index defined from depth-weighted bid/ask prices and index price, not the signed price-difference Basis required by the canonical contract. The reviewed official guide did not establish a matching historical OI endpoint.
- **Bitget:** Funding history and current OI fields are documented, but the reviewed public documentation does not establish historical OI coverage and a direct Basis fact together for the required instruments. Mark/index prices are not substituted for Basis.
- **Gate.io:** Funding history and historical contract statistics document OI in contracts and quote-currency value. No direct provider-supplied Basis field or endpoint was found in the inspected official Futures API guide.
- **MEXC:** The previously documented gaps remain: BTC_USDT Funding is documented, but authoritative canonical OI semantics and direct Basis are not established in the inspected official documentation.
- **Binance COIN-M:** Funding, OI and Basis are documented within COIN-M, but BTCUSD COIN-M remains incompatible as a substitute for BTCUSDT USDⓈ-M/Spot.

No provider/product is selected for the Project Owner. No implementation, live provider API call, runtime/VPS operation, protected contract/schema change, or STEP-P5-007 activation occurred. The Checkpoint’s available_persisted_claim: NONE is preserved. PRQ-3 remains a required unresolved capability; this B disposition is not delivery of PRQ-3 and does not make S-04 available.

## 3. Evidence classification and research method

Each finding below is classified as one of:

- **Official provider fact:** explicit endpoint, response field, unit, definition, timestamp or limit stated in official provider documentation.
- **Repository fact:** directly present in a retrieved repository contract, requirement, persistence record or governed artifact.
- **Not established:** the reviewed official sources do not prove the required claim. This does not prove the provider lacks the capability.
- **Inference rejected:** a plausible interpretation that is not promoted to an authoritative provider guarantee.
- **Identity-incompatible:** a route exists but its product/instrument identity cannot substitute for the required identity.

## 8. Temporal semantics, coverage, provenance and failure handling

### 8.1 Temporal semantics and coverage

- Binance USDⓈ-M Funding uses fundingTime (milliseconds); current OI time is transaction time; historical OI timestamp is period end; Basis timestamp is period start.
- Bybit Funding history uses fundingRateTimestamp (milliseconds); OI history timestamp is milliseconds; the reviewed ticker schema does not establish a distinct Basis event timestamp.
- OKX Funding and OI timestamps are milliseconds; premium-history ts is the premium-index data-generation time, not Basis time.
- Bitget Funding and OI timestamps are milliseconds in the reviewed API; historical OI coverage was not established.
- Gate.io Funding and OI statistics use Unix timestamps in seconds; provider timestamp resolution and semantics must be preserved rather than silently converted into another metric’s event time.
- MEXC current and historical Funding have distinct timestamp fields; no Basis timestamp can be assigned without a direct Basis source.

S-04 requires ordered prior observations for funding velocity and OI delta. DOC-P4-003 explicitly requires missing prior observations to remain INSUFFICIENT_HISTORY, not zero; zero or negative elapsed time is invalid. No provider history limit above proves gap-free cadence or current runtime availability. Future implementation must handle pagination, duplicate/replayed records, gaps, stale data, missing prior observations and contradictions explicitly.

### 8.2 Provenance and raw-source semantics

The reviewed providers expose provider identity, instrument/symbol identifiers and event/period timestamps in their respective endpoint schemas. This supports designing a traceable future adapter but does not prove an implemented adapter, persistence success, or authoritative S-04 read-back. The original provider payload and field names must be retained so unit, product-family, timestamp and transformation semantics remain auditable. A mark price, index price, premium index, fair price, funding payment, or contract count must not be relabeled as another metric.
