## 1. Executive determination

**Primary disposition: B — NO QUALIFIED ROUTE ESTABLISHED.**

The continued investigation improved the evidence base but did not establish one compatible route that authoritatively supplies Funding, Open Interest (OI), and direct Basis for both exact required instrument scopes, BTCUSDT and SOLUSDT, with compatible units, timestamps, coverage, provenance, and existing-contract representation.

The earlier Binance USDⓈ-M OI gap remains unresolved after targeted inspection of the exact official legacy Chinese documentation entry and the current official Chinese USDⓈ-M Market Data page. The legacy URL now redirects to the current derivatives documentation landing page rather than exposing a retrievable, version-pinned legacy page; the retrieval limitation is recorded in §4.1. The current Chinese page describes historical sumOpenInterest as “持仓总数量” (total holding quantity) and sumOpenInterestValue as “持仓总价值” (total holding value), and identifies the historical timestamp as period end; current openInterest time is described as matching-engine time. Those labels narrow the gap but do not specify the quantity unit, aggregation/calculation method, valuation currency/basis, or authoritative relationship between the quantity and value fields. Official Binance product references do exist for SOLUSDT USDⓈ-M perpetual futures, and Binance’s funding FAQ explicitly names SOLUSDT in its funding-rate material. These improve product-specific evidence but do not establish all three metrics for that exact product identity. Current listing/runtime availability remains outside this Task Order.

Additional candidates were investigated using official provider documentation:

- **Bybit:** Funding history and historical OI are documented, and the ticker schema includes basis and basisRate. However, the ticker schema does not establish the precise meaning/units and reliable non-empty historical Basis coverage for the exact BTCUSDT and SOLUSDT perpetual products. The ticker’s response-level time does not establish a metric-specific Basis event timestamp.
- **OKX:** Funding history and explicit current OI fields in contracts, coin quantity and USD value are documented. The official premium-history metric is a premium index defined from depth-weighted bid/ask prices and index price, not the signed price-difference Basis required by the canonical contract. The route `GET /api/v5/rubik/stat/contracts/open-interest-history` is corroborated by the official endpoint index and OKX SDK surface, but was not found in the directly inspected main API-guide page body; this establishes endpoint existence only, not response semantics, exact-instrument qualification, historical backfill, timestamp boundaries, or Basis compatibility.
- **Bitget:** Funding history and current OI fields are documented, but the reviewed public documentation does not establish historical OI coverage and a direct Basis fact together for the required instruments. Mark/index prices are not substituted for Basis.
- **Gate.io:** Funding history and historical contract statistics document OI in contracts and quote-currency value. No direct provider-supplied Basis field or endpoint was found in the inspected official Futures API guide.
- **MEXC:** The previously documented gaps remain: BTC_USDT Funding is documented, but authoritative canonical OI semantics and direct Basis are not established in the inspected official documentation.
- **Binance COIN-M:** Funding, OI and Basis are documented within COIN-M, but BTCUSD COIN-M remains incompatible as a substitute for BTCUSDT USDⓈ-M/Spot.

No provider/product is selected for the Project Owner. The existing derivatives normalizer remains Binance/MEXC-only. No USDⓈ-M Futures adapter/normalization implementation, provider selection, live provider API call, runtime/VPS/SentinelX operation, protected contract/schema change, or STEP-P5-007 activation occurred. The Checkpoint’s `available_persisted_claim: NONE` is preserved. PRQ-3 remains a required unresolved capability; this B disposition is not delivery of PRQ-3 and does not make S-04 available.

## 3. Evidence classification and research method

Each finding below is classified as one of:

- **Official provider fact:** explicit endpoint, response field, unit, definition, timestamp or limit stated in official provider documentation.
- **Repository fact:** directly present in a retrieved repository contract, requirement, persistence record or governed artifact.
- **Not established:** the reviewed official sources do not prove the required claim. This does not prove the provider lacks the capability.
- **Inference rejected:** a plausible interpretation that is not promoted to an authoritative provider guarantee.
- **Identity-incompatible:** a route exists but its product/instrument identity cannot substitute for the required identity.

Official documentation was retrieved on 2026-10-09. Direct source URLs are recorded below. No sample payload was treated as a live observation, and no third-party data or commentary was used to fill missing semantics.
