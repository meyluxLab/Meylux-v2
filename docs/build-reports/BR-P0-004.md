> Provider-specific integrations/adapters MUST remain isolated behind the approved provider boundary and MUST NOT bypass or alter authoritative V2 contracts, evidence rules, deterministic-baseline rules, or governance boundaries. Purpose: make provider isolation explicit and unambiguous without redesigning provider architecture.

### 2.2 `INV-V2-007` — Single Authoritative Persistence

A new explicit constitutional boundary was added after the existing evidence-discipline/V1 boundaries:

> For each governed durable state domain, the designated authoritative persistence boundary MUST be explicit and singular. Competing authoritative persistence, or silent divergence between purported authoritative stores, is prohibited. Transport/cache infrastructure, including Redis, MUST NOT become a competing permanent source of truth.

Purpose: make the singular authoritative persistence boundary and prohibition of competing permanent truth explicit.

### 2.3 `INV-V2-009` — Zero Lookahead

A new explicit constitutional boundary was added:

> Future or otherwise unavailable-at-decision-time information MUST NOT contaminate an as-of analytical state, historical evaluation, or deterministic computation. Historical and deterministic results MUST use only information available at the relevant decision/evaluation time.

Purpose: make temporal/as-of integrity explicit and prohibit future-information contamination.

### 2.4 `INV-V2-010` — Evidence-Backed State

A new explicit constitutional boundary was added:

> Authoritative state, completion, status, and verification claims MUST be grounded in actual available validated evidence. Unsupported state assertions MUST NOT be represented as executed, complete, accepted, or verified.

Purpose: make evidence-backed state claims explicit and prevent unsupported lifecycle assertions.
