## 4. Verification / Evidence / Gate Findings

### 4.1 Gate lifecycle

The reconciled gate model explicitly distinguishes:

```text
DEFINED
→ ENTRY SATISFIED
→ EVIDENCE ASSEMBLED
→ AUDIT / VERIFICATION
→ ACCEPTED / CLOSED
```

A gate is not closed merely because its requirements are documented or because a Producer reports completion.

### 4.2 Evidence classes

The reconciled model distinguishes:

- structural evidence;
- behavioral evidence;
- test evidence;
- operational evidence;
- governance evidence;
- data / analytical evidence.

The model explicitly prevents one evidence class from silently substituting for another.

### 4.3 Provenance and traceability

Material evidence is required to retain applicable source reference, subject, affected Stable IDs, parent Task Order / Step / Phase, producer or executor, time/version context, and verification disposition. Commit identifiers or hashes may establish content/version lineage where applicable but do not by themselves prove execution or correctness.

### 4.4 Independence

Producer evidence remains non-independent. `ROL-V2-001 — CONTROL / REVIEWER` remains the independent audit and verification authority for the Producer artifact chain unless a higher-authority process explicitly assigns another verifier.

Operator execution evidence remains distinct from Producer evidence.

### 4.5 Lifecycle distinctions

The reconciled policy preserves distinct states including:

```text
DESIGNED / DRAFT / PROPOSED / APPROVED / IMPLEMENTED / EXECUTED /
TESTED / VERIFIED / ACCEPTED / RATIFIED / FROZEN / CLOSED
```

The model explicitly prevents transitions such as `IMPLEMENTED → VERIFIED` or `VERIFIED → ACCEPTED` without the required evidence and authority.

### 4.6 Acceptance, ratification, freeze, and closure

The reconciled definitions distinguish:

- verification of evidence against a verification method;
- acceptance by the authorized acceptance authority;
- ratification as a formal governance decision;
- freeze as a controlled immutability boundary;
- closure as the recorded satisfaction of the applicable completion protocol.

These are not interchangeable states.

### 4.7 Analytical/data evidence vs governance evidence

The model explicitly separates market/data/analytical evidence from governance evidence. A market-data replay cannot prove Task Order authorization, and a governance approval cannot prove a market-data claim or runtime behavior.

### 4.8 Failure, missing, stale, and contradictory evidence

The reconciled policy requires missing, failed, stale, unavailable, insufficient, unauthorized, or contradictory evidence to remain explicit. Such evidence cannot silently become positive verification evidence.

### 4.9 Gate closure requirements

Gate closure requires the applicable identity/scope, entry criteria evidence, required evidence classes, provenance/version context, blocker disposition, independent verification, authorized acceptance/closure decision, resulting state transition, and lineage. Missing required evidence is a gap, not an implicit pass.

### 4.10 Safety and authority boundary

The reconciled artifacts explicitly state that verification/gate semantics do not authorize runtime, deployment, V1/VPS, market, trading, capital, fund-transfer, or provider-runtime activity, and do not activate later Phase 0 Steps.
