# Meylux V2 — Evidence Policy

Status: Draft — Pre-Phase-0

Claims such as IMPLEMENTED, TESTED, VERIFIED, ACCEPTED, COMPLETE, GREEN, or CLOSED require identifiable evidence.

Evidence may include:
- CI/test output
- controlled execution output
- operator EXEC-LOG
- replay results
- production verification results
- recorded hashes/commit identifiers where applicable

Unproven claims must remain UNVERIFIED.
