## 3. Required Outcome

### 3.1 S-02 — Market Structure

S-02 must deterministically consume the authoritative persisted structural events/zones and provide, as applicable:

- dominant structural state while preserving `UNCONFIRMED`;
- latest confirmed structural event whose authoritative `knowledge_time <= snapshot.as_of`;
- deterministic relationship of current price to the protected structural level using the governed Decimal policy;
- nearest unmitigated applicable zones;
- explicit `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` / unavailable outcomes where the authoritative fact surface is insufficient.

S-02 must not recompute or redefine P4 structural mathematics.

### 3.2 S-11 — Price Action

S-11 must deterministically evaluate closed-candle price-action patterns required by the ratified roadmap:

- `PIN_BAR`;
- `ENGULFING`;
- `INSIDE_BAR`;
- `ZONE_REJECTION`.

`pattern_time` must correspond to the closed candle boundary required by the semantic contract.

Zone rejection must resolve against authoritative persisted zones rather than a P5-created competing zone model.

### 3.3 S-12 — Liquidity

S-12 must deterministically provide:

- nearest applicable liquidity zones;
- `SWEPT` / `UNSWEPT` state using candles occurring after zone formation;
- explicit `PARTIAL` behavior where depth evidence is unavailable;
- depth-dependent interpretation only when authoritative snapshot depth is actually present.

No unsupported depth fact may be inferred from symbol, provider, source code, table existence, or configuration.
