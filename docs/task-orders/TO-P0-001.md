# MEYLUX V2 — FORMAL TASK ORDER

**Task Order ID:** `TO-P0-001`
**Phase:** `PH-P0`
**Step:** `STEP-P0-001`
**Task:** Master Architecture Reconciliation
**Issuer:** CONTROL / REVIEWER
**Recipient:** PRODUCER / ARCHITECT-BUILDER
**Status:** `AUTHORIZED TO START`
**Entry Gate:** `G-0R` (`RATIFIED / VERIFIED`)
**Entry Review:** `AR-P0-ENTRY-001`

## 1. Objective

Perform the formal reconciliation of `DOC-V2-ARCH-001` / `MASTER_ARCHITECTURE_V2.md` against the approved V2 formation knowledge and authoritative governance/evidence inputs, producing the evidence required for final architecture ratification/freeze consideration.

## 2. Governing Inputs

Use, at minimum:

- V2 Constitution and architectural invariants.
- `DOC-V2-ARCH-001` Master Architecture V2 draft baseline.
- Governance and Role Contracts.
- Artifact Protocol.
- Continuity Bootstrap and Transfer State.
- `CURRENT_CHECKPOINT.json`.
- `OPEN_QUESTIONS.yaml`.
- `DEFERRED_DECISIONS.yaml`.
- Relevant ADR/ACR decisions.
- Phase/Step Registry records.
- Relevant approved formation artifacts and evidence.
- Relevant post-baseline project evidence where available and authoritative.

## 3. Required Work

1. Identify material contradictions, omissions, unsupported assumptions, and architectural inconsistencies.
2. Trace each material finding to authoritative source evidence.
3. Distinguish architecture requirements from implementation choices and historical observations.
4. Preserve all V2 architectural invariants and the V1 isolation boundary.
5. Identify required ADR/ACR/change-control items rather than silently resolving material architectural conflicts.
6. Produce a reconciliation result suitable for CONTROL / REVIEWER audit.

## 4. Required Deliverables

- Producer reconciliation artifact/report with stable identity and traceability to `TO-P0-001`.
- Explicit finding/disposition matrix for material issues.
- Explicit list of proposed architectural changes, if any, separated from already-authorized architecture.
- Self-test/consistency evidence actually performed by the Producer.
- Build Report identifying actual work performed, files changed, and evidence produced.

## 5. Constraints

- Do not implement runtime/application functionality.
- Do not modify V1 or V1 VPS/environment.
- Do not silently ratify/freeze the Master Architecture.
- Do not silently create/change Stable IDs, contracts, governance rules, or architectural invariants.
- Do not fabricate evidence, results, market data, verification state, or completion claims.
- Do not expand scope into future phases.

## 6. Stop Conditions

STOP THAT PART and escalate to CONTROL if:

- a genuine architecture/governance conflict requires a formal decision;
- a Stable ID or authoritative identity is uncertain;
- required evidence cannot be established;
- completion requires changing a higher-authority rule;
- the work would exceed Phase 0 / `STEP-P0-001` scope.

## 7. Acceptance Criteria

CONTROL / REVIEWER must be able to verify that:

- reconciliation covers the required authoritative inputs;
- material conflicts/omissions/assumptions are explicitly identified;
- each material finding has evidence and disposition/status;
- no unauthorized architecture change was introduced;
- proposed changes are routed through ADR/ACR/change control where required;
- the Build Report is truthful and traceable;
- no unsupported verification/completion claim is made.

## 8. Artifact Chain

`TO-P0-001 → BUILD-REPORT → AUDIT-REPORT → EXEC-LOG (if applicable) → CURRENT_CHECKPOINT`

This Task Order authorizes the reconciliation work only. It does not ratify or freeze the Master Architecture.
