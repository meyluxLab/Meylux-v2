## 1. Authority and objective

The Project Owner has expressly rejected carrying the existing PRQ-4 limitation forward merely by reclassifying it as `UNAVAILABLE`, documenting it, or accepting it as a permanent limitation for the purpose of crossing the STEP-P5-003 boundary.

The required outcome is:

`PRQ-4 = RESOLVED`

The objective is to establish the underlying governed capability required by the Phase-5 S-10 surface: authoritative, persisted, provenance-complete quality/acquisition evidence that can be read back deterministically and can legitimately participate in the P5 evidence model.

This Task Order is outcome- and requirement-driven. It deliberately does not prescribe the implementation mechanism, schema shape, migration design, contract decomposition, persistence API, evidence-record identity, or exact code changes.

The Producer retains implementation-level design authority strictly inside this Task Order.

## 2. Current root-cause baseline established by CONTROL

CONTROL has independently inspected the current Source of Truth and established the following starting facts:

1. P3 already computes explicit `DataQualityState` values including `VALID`, `DEGRADED`, `STALE`, `INCOMPLETE`, `CONTRADICTORY`, `REJECTED`, and `UNAVAILABLE`.
2. P3 quality assessment already preserves reason codes, provenance, source-record identity and lineage information at the in-memory quality boundary.
3. `QuarantineRecord` is explicitly documented as a bounded routing payload, not authoritative persistence.
4. `CanonicalRecord` accepts only `VALID` quality evidence into canonical persistence.
5. The existing `meylux.data_quality_logs` table can structurally represent quality evidence, but the current `CanonicalPersistence.persist()` path writes that log only when a canonical record has first been inserted successfully.
6. Consequently, non-`VALID` quality states that are routed away from canonical truth do not currently have a complete authoritative persisted evidence path for later read-back.
7. The current `data_quality_logs` representation does not itself establish the complete P5 `EvidenceRef` surface. In particular, the current repository representation lacks a dedicated authoritative `knowledge_time` and does not establish the complete identity/temporal/venue/timeframe evidence required by the P5 S-10 boundary.
8. `logged_at` is an operational persistence timestamp and MUST NOT be silently promoted to `knowledge_time`.
9. P5-002 deliberately rejected fabrication or substitution of missing upstream knowledge-time/quality evidence and recorded `OQ-P5-002-PRQ4-QUALITY-EVIDENCE` as OPEN.
10. The Phase-5 roadmap identifies S-10 as dependent on PRQ-4 persisted quality/acquisition evidence and requires a structured EvidenceRef resolvable to persisted evidence.

These findings identify the current capability gap, but they do not prescribe the final implementation. Producer must validate the complete chain before changing it.

## 3. Required end state

The implementation must establish a governed upstream evidence capability such that, for applicable P3 quality/acquisition outcomes:

- the authoritative quality state is persisted, including non-`VALID` states where they are semantically required;
- the persisted evidence is traceable to the originating acquisition/validation evidence;
- the evidence has deterministic identity and duplicate/retry semantics;
- provenance and lineage are preserved;
- event-time semantics are preserved independently from operational persistence time;
- an authoritative and reproducible `knowledge_time` semantic exists where the evidence model requires it;
- missing, contradictory or unsupported knowledge-time evidence is not fabricated;
- the persisted representation is read-back resolvable through the P5 EvidenceRef requirements;
- the evidence remains append-only and cannot be silently rewritten;
- canonical truth remains restricted to canonical-eligible `VALID` data;
- rejected/unavailable/degraded/etc. evidence remains distinguishable rather than being promoted or erased;
- replay, retry, duplicate delivery and restart behavior preserve the same semantic evidence;
- the resulting capability is usable by S-10 without weakening P5-002.

A migration, column, table, object, fixture, or test passing in isolation is NOT evidence of PRQ-4 resolution.

## 4. Required semantic investigation before implementation

Producer MUST first inspect the complete affected chain and determine the actual semantic model:

`P2 acquisition evidence → P3 validation → P3 quality assessment → quarantine/routing → authoritative quality evidence persistence → read-back → P5 EvidenceRef / S-10`

The investigation must determine, at minimum:

1. what exactly constitutes the quality/acquisition fact;
2. which upstream evidence establishes its event time;
3. what information establishes its knowledge time;
4. whether that knowledge time is source-derived, validation-derived, or otherwise authoritative;
5. where that information currently exists;
6. where it is currently lost;
7. whether the required semantic can be established entirely within the P3 ownership boundary;
8. whether any P2 contract/provider information is genuinely indispensable;
9. how identity, provenance, lineage, timeframe and venue are established;
10. how historical legacy observations must be classified;
11. how new evidence differs from legacy evidence whose knowledge semantics cannot be established;
12. how contradictory, malformed, missing, late and duplicate evidence is represented.

If the root-cause analysis proves that a protected P2 contract or another higher-authority boundary must change, Producer MUST NOT silently cross that boundary. The affected portion must be returned to CONTROL with the exact required change-control action. CONTROL will then establish the appropriate governed ownership path.

## 5. Implementation-level design freedom

Producer may choose the appropriate implementation method, including—where justified—changes to:

- evidence identity construction;

This list is illustrative, not exhaustive.

Producer MUST NOT assume that the existing `data_quality_logs` table is necessarily sufficient, nor assume that a new table is necessary. The final design must be derived from the actual semantic and architectural requirements.

No competing truth store may be introduced.

## 6. Mandatory semantic rules

The implementation MUST preserve the following distinctions unless authoritative evidence proves a legitimate equality:

- source/provider event time;
- observation/receipt time;
- validation time;
- persistence/logging time;
- wall-clock execution time;
- `knowledge_time`.

In particular:

- `logged_at` MUST NOT become `knowledge_time` merely because it is available;
- current wall-clock time MUST NOT be used as historical knowledge time;
- `event_time` MUST NOT be copied into `knowledge_time` unless the authoritative semantic proves that equality for the specific evidence family;
- absence of an explicit knowledge boundary MUST remain detectable;
- no timestamp may be invented to make a record eligible for P5.

For every persisted evidence family, Producer must document the semantic rule that establishes `knowledge_time`, its earliest legitimate boundary, and the evidence supporting it.

## 7. Persistence and identity requirements

The resulting authoritative evidence persistence must, as applicable:

1. preserve stable record identity;
2. preserve deterministic evidence identity;
3. preserve source-record identity;
4. preserve provenance and lineage;
5. preserve quality state and reason codes;
6. preserve event time separately from knowledge time;
7. preserve timeframe and venue where applicable to P5 evidence resolution;
8. support deterministic read-back;
9. support duplicate-safe retry;
10. support idempotent replay;
11. prevent UPDATE/DELETE mutation of authoritative historical evidence;
12. preserve atomicity between related evidence writes where atomicity is semantically required;
13. make partial failure behavior explicit;
14. preserve UTC/timezone correctness;
15. remain provider-neutral at the canonical/evidence contract boundary;
16. maintain least-privilege application access;
17. be safe for populated historical data;
18. define the treatment of legacy rows;
19. avoid destructive historical rewrites solely to increase P5 availability;
20. avoid silent promotion of non-authoritative evidence.

If a schema migration is required, it must be additive/compatible where possible, deterministic, idempotent, non-destructive, directly tested, and explicit about legacy rows and their evidence status.

Frozen historical migrations MUST NOT be rewritten.

## 8. Quality-state coverage

The implementation must explicitly address the complete quality-state space that the existing P3 contract can produce.

At minimum, demonstrate correct handling for:

- `VALID`;
- `DEGRADED`;
- `STALE`;
- `INCOMPLETE`;
- `CONTRADICTORY`;
- `REJECTED`;
- `UNAVAILABLE`.

For every non-`VALID` state that is within the required PRQ-4 evidence boundary:

- the state must be persistable as evidence;
- its reason(s) must remain explicit;
- its provenance must remain traceable;
- it must not become canonical truth;
- its EvidenceRef must resolve if the state is presented to P5/S-10;
- if authoritative knowledge time is genuinely unavailable, the implementation must not fabricate it.

A mere absence of an exception is not evidence of quality-state persistence.

## 9. P5 compatibility requirements

The implementation MUST satisfy the existing P5-002 evidence model rather than weaken it.

For every evidence record admitted as P5-resolvable, demonstrate:

`source_family`  
`record_id`  
`identity_hash`  
`event_time`  
`knowledge_time`  
`timeframe`  
`venue`

and demonstrate:

- `knowledge_time <= snapshot.as_of` → eligible;
- `knowledge_time > snapshot.as_of` → excluded;
- missing authoritative `knowledge_time` → unavailable/rejected;
- mismatched identity/hash → rejected;
- mismatched record/timeframe/venue metadata → rejected;
- duplicate identical evidence → deterministic/idempotent;
- contradictory evidence for the same logical fact → explicit contradiction, never silent selection.

P5-002 semantics MUST NOT be weakened or modified merely to accommodate this correction.

## 10. Historical and legacy evidence

Producer MUST classify existing historical evidence into at least:

1. directly authoritative quality evidence with valid temporal semantics;
2. deterministically reconstructible quality evidence;
3. physically present but semantically incomplete evidence;
4. evidence for which the required knowledge boundary cannot legitimately be established.

Only classes 1 and 2 may be promoted to authoritative P5-eligible evidence.

Classes 3 and 4 must remain explicitly non-eligible and must not be relabelled merely to satisfy the acceptance criteria.

Existing canonical data and established P3/G-3 historical evidence must not be destructively rewritten.

If a backfill is technically required, it must prove its source evidence and deterministic reconstruction rule record-by-record; otherwise the row remains unevidenced.

## 11. Edge cases and failure behavior

- missing quality assessment;
- missing provenance;
- missing source record;
- missing lineage;
- missing/malformed timestamps;
- timezone normalization;
- event-time / knowledge-time divergence;
- knowledge time exactly equal to snapshot `as_of`;
- knowledge time after `as_of`;
- unsupported provider capability;
- provider unavailable/disconnected/rate-limited states;
- stale evidence;
- contradictory validation;
- duplicate/repeated observation;
- duplicate persistence retry;
- partial transaction failure;
- restart/recovery;
- serialization/deserialization;
- concurrent duplicate attempts;
- legacy/new evidence coexistence;
- migration rerun;
- append-only mutation attempts.

No edge case may be "handled" by silently inventing a value.

## 12. Replay, determinism and provenance proof

Producer must demonstrate that the resulting evidence semantics survive:

- repeated quality assessment from identical authoritative inputs;
- persistence retry;
- duplicate delivery;
- service/process restart where applicable;
- serialization/deserialization;
- database read-back;
- replay;
- legacy/new mixed populations.

Repeated execution against identical authoritative input must not generate contradictory evidence identity or knowledge-time semantics.

Every P5 EvidenceRef produced from the resulting evidence must resolve to a real persisted record and must not rely on a synthetic identifier.

## 14. Acceptance criteria

CONTROL may consider PRQ-4 technically resolved only after independent verification can establish all of the following:

1. The root cause is explicitly identified and agrees with repository evidence.
2. The underlying persisted-quality-evidence capability exists in the authoritative path.
3. Required non-`VALID` quality/acquisition evidence is actually persisted and recoverable.
4. The persisted evidence is provenance- and lineage-complete.
5. Deterministic identity/hash semantics are established and tested.
6. `event_time` and `knowledge_time` remain semantically distinct unless equality is explicitly justified.
7. `knowledge_time` has a real authoritative derivation rule; `logged_at`/wall-clock substitution is absent.
8. Missing or unsupported knowledge evidence remains explicitly ineligible rather than fabricated.
9. P5 EvidenceRefs resolve to actual persisted evidence.
10. `knowledge_time <= as_of` eligibility and `knowledge_time > as_of` exclusion are proven.
11. Canonical persistence remains restricted to `VALID` data.
12. Replay, retry, duplicate and restart semantics are deterministic.
13. Contradictory evidence is explicit and never silently repaired.
14. Historical legacy treatment is evidence-based and non-destructive.
15. Append-only/security boundaries remain intact.
16. Relevant unit/integration/CI evidence is real and reproducible.
17. No P5-002 weakening or P5-003 implementation has been used to manufacture success.
18. No frozen architecture or contract has been silently changed.
19. The Build Report contains complete evidence, limitations, deviations and exact implementation revision.
20. CONTROL can independently reproduce the conclusion from repository/runtime evidence.

The target disposition after successful independent verification is:

`PRQ-4 = RESOLVED`

not `UNAVAILABLE / ACCEPTED LIMITATION`.
