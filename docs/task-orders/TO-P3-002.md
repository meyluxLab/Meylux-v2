Establish structural, schema and initial identity validation for incoming acquisition data within the already-established canonical contract, identity and validation foundation of `STEP-P3-001`.

P3-002 consumes and enforces the verified foundation established by P3-001. It must not recreate, replace, weaken, duplicate, or silently reinterpret that foundation.

## 4. Required Work

Within the authorized boundary, establish deterministic structural, schema and initial identity validation for incoming acquisition evidence, including only responsibilities supported by existing authoritative contracts and validation semantics.

Required work includes, where applicable and directly supported by the existing contracts:

1. structural shape validation;
2. schema/conformance validation against already-authorized canonical or acquisition contract definitions;
3. required-field and permitted-field structural validation;
4. type and nullability enforcement where already defined by authoritative contracts;
5. initial identity validation using the identity foundation established by P3-001;
6. deterministic rejection/reporting of structurally or schematically invalid evidence;
7. preservation of provider-neutral acquisition boundaries;
8. deterministic validation evidence sufficient for independent CONTROL audit.

P3-002 must not invent or redefine schemas, contracts, interfaces, requirements, or Stable IDs.

## 5. Existing Contract and Architecture Preservation

The Producer must preserve `DOC-V2-ARCH-001`, all frozen architecture, existing Stable IDs, Phase 2 acquisition contracts, and the verified P3-001 foundation.

Provider-specific wire aliases, transport fields, sequence details and provider identifiers must remain outside canonical semantics unless an explicit authoritative domain requirement already establishes their promotion.

If implementation requires modification of a frozen architecture, contract, Stable ID, schema definition, or governance boundary: **STOP THAT PART** and report the exact conflict to CONTROL.

## 6. Required Implementation Boundary

P3-002 is limited to structural, schema and initial identity validation.

It may enforce existing authoritative structural/schema/identity constraints. It may not create a new contract or redefine an existing contract merely to make implementation convenient.

P3-002 must not implement temporal, sequence, completeness, continuity, market-semantic price/spread/precision, normalization, provider mapping, cross-venue equivalence, quarantine/DLQ, lineage, persistence, or normalized event-handoff behavior owned by later Phase 3 boundaries.

The Producer must not implement `STEP-P3-003` or any later Step, Phase 4, technical indicators, market structure, order flow, specialist intelligence, AI interpretation, Opportunity Score, trade ideas, trading/order execution, capital/custody/leverage/transfer/withdrawal operations, V1 mutation/integration, unauthorized VPS activity, architecture redesign, or unrelated cleanup/refactoring.

## 7. Deterministic Data Rules

- Validation behavior must be deterministic and reproducible.
- Existing approved numeric and precision doctrine must be preserved; P3-002 must not introduce a new quantitative doctrine.
- Missing, invalid, contradictory, stale, unavailable, or insufficient evidence must not be fabricated or silently repaired.
- No NaN/Inf may leak into authoritative outputs.
- No silent divide-by-zero or equivalent invalid mathematical behavior.
- No fabricated fallback market values.
- Validation findings and outcomes must remain evidence-based and traceable to actual input evidence.
- Pure contract/validation logic must not depend on network, wall clock, mutable global state, filesystem or database I/O unless explicitly required by an existing authoritative contract.
