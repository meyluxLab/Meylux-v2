## 3. Sole investigation question

**Does at least one specific, precisely identified provider/product route authoritatively support all three required facts—Funding, OI, and Basis—with compatible instrument identity, units, temporal semantics, provenance, coverage, and existing-contract representation; if not, what exact evidence gap or protected-boundary conflict prevents qualification?**

The Producer must investigate candidates without choosing one on behalf of the Owner. Candidate discovery is not qualification. Partial support for one or two facts is not complete route qualification.

## 4. Required route-by-route evidence

For every candidate evaluated, record:

### 4.1 Exact provider and product identity
- Provider/product API family and exact market/product family (for example, do not conflate Binance COIN-M with USDⓈ-M, or Spot with Futures).
- Instrument/symbol identity, contract type, settlement/quote/base assets where applicable, and provider fields needed to distinguish instruments unambiguously.
- Exact public endpoint/channel and operation, official documentation URL, documented API version or page revision where available, retrieval date, and relevant schema/field names.
- Whether evidence is an explicit provider guarantee, documented endpoint/schema fact, sample-only observation, repository fact, or inference. Inference must never be promoted to provider guarantee.

### 4.2 Funding, OI, and Basis individually
For each required fact, document:
- Availability: established, not established, unsupported, or explicitly unavailable.
- Exact provider source/endpoint and product-family scope.
- Meaning, units, sign convention, scaling, and whether the value is provider-supplied or locally derived.
- Instrument/product identity and any cross-instrument relationships required.
- Event/observation timestamp, publication/update cadence, receipt/knowledge-time implications, historical versus current coverage, retention limits, and staleness/latency limits where authoritative evidence exists.
- Provenance and raw source fields needed to preserve lineage.
- Failure, missing-field, unsupported-product, rate-limit, maintenance, and contradictory-source semantics.
- Exact limitations and any semantics not established by the provider.

Basis must not be silently replaced by a locally calculated proxy. A fair-price field or a formula mentioned in provider documentation does not automatically establish a canonical Basis fact. Any derived value requires a separately authoritative, existing semantic definition and proof that its inputs and product identity satisfy that definition; otherwise record the gap.

### 4.3 Complete-route compatibility
Determine whether Funding, OI, and Basis coexist on one compatible, concrete provider/product route. Explain any difference in instrument, contract type, endpoint, timestamp, units, temporal coverage, or provenance and whether existing contracts can preserve it without ambiguity or loss of truth.

A route is not qualified if any required metric is missing or only inferred; a product-specific guarantee is generalized to another product family; timestamps cannot support ordering, staleness, and no-lookahead; identity is ambiguous; provenance cannot be traced to authoritative evidence; historical/live semantics are silently conflated; incompatible instruments or venues are substituted without an authoritative rule; or existing canonical/evidence contracts cannot represent the facts truthfully.

## 6. Required definitive disposition

The Build Report must select exactly one primary outcome, with evidence:

- **A — QUALIFIED ROUTE:** At least one specific provider/product route is demonstrated by authoritative evidence to satisfy Funding, OI, and Basis together with compatible identity, units, temporal semantics, provenance, coverage, and existing-contract/persistence representation. Name and qualify the route(s), but do not choose one for implementation on the Owner's behalf.
- **B — NO QUALIFIED ROUTE ESTABLISHED:** Available authoritative evidence does not prove a complete compatible route. List each candidate and each metric's status, exact missing provider facts/semantics, why the gaps prevent qualification, what can truthfully be preserved, and objective evidence/reconsideration conditions. Do not present this as delivery or permanent removal of PRQ-3.
- **C — CHANGE CONTROL REQUIRED:** A specific, evidenced conflict with a frozen architecture invariant, authoritative contract/schema, Stable ID, or governance boundary prevents a viable route from being represented under existing authority. Identify the exact protected item, conflicting requirement/evidence, why the conflict cannot be resolved within existing semantics, and the required formal decision/change-control path. Do not implement or treat an assumed amendment as approved.

Choose C only where a demonstrated protected-boundary conflict actually requires change control; otherwise choose A if a complete route is qualified, or B if none is. Do not use C merely because implementation would be nontrivial or evidence is incomplete.

These are investigation dispositions only. None authorizes derivatives implementation, provider/product selection, schema/contract change, or Phase/Step activation.

## 7. Failure, contradiction, and edge-case coverage

Explicitly consider, where relevant: undocumented or ambiguous fields; missing/malformed/non-numeric/non-finite values; unsupported symbols, contract families, or endpoint coverage; duplicate, replayed, non-monotonic, stale, delayed, or contradictory observations; event-time versus publication-time versus receipt/knowledge-time ambiguity; REST/WebSocket or endpoint-version disagreement; rate limits, outages, maintenance, retention limits, and historical/live mismatch; unit, scale, sign, interval, settlement asset, and contract multiplier differences; Funding and OI from one product family combined with Basis from another; proxy/derived basis mistaken for a provider-supplied canonical fact; endpoint existence mistaken for proof of required fields or semantics; provider documentation changes or insufficient version pinning; and persistence success mistaken for semantic eligibility.

Classify each gap as a provider-evidence gap, product coverage gap, semantic/identity incompatibility, repository implementation gap, or protected architecture/contract conflict. Do not invent data, silently repair contradictory facts, or use synthetic observations as authoritative market evidence.
