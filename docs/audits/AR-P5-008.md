## 3. Enforcement-Gap Finding

The normative principle itself was already sufficient in ADR-GOVERNANCE-014 and ARTIFACT_PROTOCOL_V2.

However, the workflow audit identified a real operational persistence gap:

- CONTROL's Task Order authority required dependencies/evidence/acceptance criteria, but did not explicitly require a No-Drop prerequisite/completion check before closure/disposition.
- Producer role rules required surfacing uncertainty and preserving unresolved material output, but did not explicitly require an unresolved required capability to remain traceable as a resolution obligation in the Build Report.
- PROJECT GUIDE prohibited false completion and hidden assumptions, but did not explicitly require preservation of an unresolved required-capability obligation when reporting current/historical state.
- AI Continuation Protocol preserved lifecycle and active-work state, but did not explicitly require successor reconstruction to carry an ADR-014 unresolved-prerequisite obligation forward.

Therefore, ADR-014 was normative but could rely too heavily on role interpretation at these workflow boundaries. This is a genuine enforcement gap, not a deficiency in the ADR itself.

## 4. Minimum Bounded Correction Applied

CONTROL added narrowly scoped operational enforcement clauses to:

1. `docs/governance/ROLE_CONTRACT_CONTROL_REVIEWER_V2.md`
   - mandatory No-Drop check for Task Order authoring, output audit, verification and closure;
   - explicit lawful completion/removal/non-required outcomes;
   - required traceability fields for unresolved prerequisites.

2. `docs/governance/ROLE_DEFINITION_PRODUCER_ARCHITECT_BUILDER_V2.md`
   - mandatory Build Report traceability for unresolved required capabilities;
   - explicit prohibition on treating degraded runtime states as capability completion;
   - dependency escalation without silent scope weakening/expansion.

3. `docs/governance/ROLE_CONTRACT_PROJECT_GUIDE_V2.md`
   - mandatory distinction between runtime limitation and capability completion;
   - preservation/surfacing of unresolved prerequisite state;
   - ambiguity routing instead of silent optionalization.

4. `docs/governance/AI_CONTINUATION_PROTOCOL_V2.md`
   - successor-session No-Drop reconstruction check;
   - explicit preservation of resolution obligations and historical evidence.

These are operational hooks only. They do not redefine ADR-014, create new lifecycle states, alter authority, or authorize implementation.

## 5. Requirement-by-Requirement Verification

### 5.1 Degraded runtime cannot equal completion

**PASS.**

The corrected workflow explicitly states that PARTIAL, INSUFFICIENT_DATA, UNAVAILABLE, UNAVAILABLE_INPUT, SKIPPED, and equivalent degraded states do not by themselves establish capability completion.

### 5.2 Missing prerequisite creates resolution obligation

**PASS.**

CONTROL and Producer workflow clauses now require unresolved prerequisites of still-required capabilities to remain explicit and traceable.

### 5.3 Traceability

**PASS.**

The operational clauses require affected capability, limitation, prerequisite, resolution path/question, responsible boundary, required evidence, and completion consequence to remain traceable as applicable.

### 5.4 Bounded corrective work

**PASS.**

The CONTROL rule explicitly limits corrective expansion to the identified prerequisite problem. Existing ADR-014 bounded-correction governance remains authoritative.

### 5.5 Formal removal only

**PASS.**

The CONTROL enforcement hook requires one of the ADR-014 lawful outcomes before closure/disposition: prerequisite resolved and capability verified; formal removal from required boundary; or authoritative evidence that the capability was not required.

### 5.6 Historical immutability

**PASS.**

All four workflow corrections explicitly preserve historical evidence and distinguish later resolution evidence from historical execution.

### 5.7 CONTROL closure behavior

**PASS.**

CONTROL's role contract now makes the No-Drop prerequisite/completion check an explicit precondition to treating affected governed work as complete/dispositioned.

### 5.8 CONTROL / PRODUCER / PROJECT GUIDE consistency

**PASS.**

All three role contracts now carry the same ADR-014 semantic boundary appropriate to their existing authority. The AI Continuation Protocol additionally preserves the obligation across successor sessions.
